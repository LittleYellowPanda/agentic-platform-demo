# Aurora European Equity Fund — Annual Report 2025 (extract)

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform.*

## Key figures

- Net assets on 31 December 2025: 1.24 billion euros (1.07 billion euros a year earlier).
- Performance of class R in 2025: **+8.3%**, against +7.1% for the MSCI Europe Net Return index.
- Portfolio turnover over the year: 38%.
- Number of holdings at year end: 61.

## Manager's commentary

2025 was marked by the recovery of European industrial companies in the second half of the year and by the gradual decline in interest rates. The Fund benefited from its overweight in capital goods and in healthcare, while its underweight in energy weighed on relative performance in the first quarter.

The largest positive contributors were a Danish pharmaceutical group, a Dutch semiconductor equipment maker and a French electrical equipment company. The main detractors were two luxury goods companies, penalised by weaker demand from Asia.

We sold our positions in two telecom operators whose debt levels we considered excessive, and we initiated a position in a Spanish renewable energy producer.
