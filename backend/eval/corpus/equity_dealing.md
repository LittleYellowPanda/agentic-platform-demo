# Aurora European Equity Fund — Subscriptions and Redemptions

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform.*

## Net asset value

The net asset value (NAV) of each share class is calculated every business day in Luxembourg, based on the closing prices of the same day, and published on the website of Northwind Asset Management the following morning.

## Cut-off time

Subscription and redemption orders received by the transfer agent before **12:00 noon Luxembourg time** on a business day are executed at the NAV of that day. Orders received after the cut-off time are executed at the NAV of the next business day. Investors therefore do not know the price at which their order will be executed when they place it (forward pricing).

## Settlement

Subscriptions and redemptions are settled within **two business days** after the valuation day (T+2).

## Swing pricing

To protect existing investors from the trading costs caused by large inflows or outflows, the Fund applies swing pricing. When net subscriptions or redemptions on a given day exceed 3% of net assets, the NAV is adjusted by a swing factor of up to 1.5%, upwards for net subscriptions and downwards for net redemptions.

## Switching

Investors may switch from one share class to another class of the Fund, or to another sub-fund of Northwind Asset Management, free of charge, provided they meet the minimum investment of the new class.

## Suspension

The management company may temporarily suspend the calculation of the NAV, and therefore subscriptions and redemptions, in exceptional circumstances, for example when a significant part of the portfolio cannot be valued because a market is closed. Investors who have placed orders are informed without delay.

## Redemption gates

If redemption requests on a single business day exceed 10% of net assets, the management company may defer the part above this threshold to the next business day.
