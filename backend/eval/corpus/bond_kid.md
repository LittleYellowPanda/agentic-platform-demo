# Aurora Euro Bond Fund, class R — Key Information Document

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform.*

## What is this product?

An open-ended investment fund (UCITS) investing in euro-denominated bonds, managed by Northwind Asset Management. Class R distributes a coupon every quarter.

## What are the risks and what could I get in return?

The summary risk indicator of this product is **2 on a scale of 1 to 7**, which is a low risk class. The recommended holding period is 3 years. The value of your investment can still fall, in particular when interest rates rise.

Performance scenarios for an investment of 10,000 euros over 3 years:

| Scenario | After 1 year | After 3 years |
|---|---|---|
| Stress | 8,640 euros | 8,710 euros |
| Unfavourable | 9,310 euros | 9,480 euros |
| Moderate | 10,180 euros | 10,560 euros |
| Favourable | 10,900 euros | 11,420 euros |

## What are the costs?

- **Entry costs:** up to 1.00% of the amount invested.
- **Exit costs:** we do not charge an exit fee for this product.
- **Ongoing charges:** 0.75% of the value of your investment per year.
- **Transaction costs:** 0.09% per year.
- **Performance fee:** there is no performance fee for this product.

For an investment of 10,000 euros held for 3 years in the moderate scenario, total costs would amount to approximately 270 euros.

## How long should I hold it and can I take money out early?

Recommended holding period: 3 years. You can redeem your shares on any business day.
