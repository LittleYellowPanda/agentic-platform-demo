# Aurora Euro Bond Fund — Prospectus (extract)

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform. Northwind Asset Management and the Aurora funds do not exist.*

## Management company

The Aurora Euro Bond Fund (the "Fund") is a sub-fund managed by Northwind Asset Management, authorised in Luxembourg. The depositary bank is Harbour Trust Bank S.A. The Fund was launched on 2 October 2012 and its reference currency is the euro.

## Investment objective

The Fund aims to generate income and moderate capital growth by investing in bonds denominated in euros. It seeks to outperform the Bloomberg Euro Aggregate index over a rolling period of three years, after fees.

## Investment policy

The Fund invests at least 80% of its net assets in bonds denominated in euros, issued by governments, supranational organisations and companies. At least 85% of the bonds must be rated investment grade (BBB- or above) at the time of purchase. Up to 15% of net assets may be invested in high-yield bonds. The Fund does not invest in equities.

The modified duration of the portfolio is kept between 3 and 8 years. Exposure to a single corporate issuer is limited to 4% of net assets; this limit does not apply to bonds issued by euro area governments.

## Use of derivatives

The Fund may use interest rate futures, interest rate swaps and credit default swaps to manage duration and credit risk. Total exposure, including derivatives, may not exceed 150% of net assets.

## Main risks

- **Interest rate risk:** when interest rates rise, the value of bonds falls. The longer the duration, the larger the fall.
- **Credit risk:** an issuer may be unable to pay interest or repay its debt.
- **Liquidity risk:** some corporate bonds can be difficult to sell quickly at a fair price.
- **Derivatives risk:** derivatives can amplify losses as well as gains.

The Fund is suitable for investors seeking regular income who plan to stay invested for at least three years.
