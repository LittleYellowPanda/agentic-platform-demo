# Aurora Euro Bond Fund — Subscriptions and Redemptions

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform.*

## Net asset value

The net asset value of each share class is calculated every business day in Luxembourg and published the following morning.

## Cut-off time

Orders received by the transfer agent before **3:00 pm Luxembourg time** on a business day are executed at the NAV of that day. Orders received after this time are executed at the NAV of the next business day.

## Settlement

Subscriptions and redemptions of the bond fund are settled **one business day** after the valuation day (T+1).

## Swing pricing

When net subscriptions or redemptions on a given day exceed 5% of net assets, the NAV is adjusted by a swing factor of up to 0.8%.

## Coupons

Class R pays a coupon every quarter, in January, April, July and October. The coupon is paid in cash to the investor's account; investors may ask their distributor to reinvest it automatically.

## Redemption gates

If redemption requests on a single business day exceed 10% of net assets, the management company may defer the part above this threshold to the next business day.
