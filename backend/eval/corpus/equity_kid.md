# Aurora European Equity Fund, class R — Key Information Document

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform.*

## What is this product?

An open-ended investment fund (UCITS) investing in European equities, managed by Northwind Asset Management. Class R accumulates its income: dividends received are reinvested in the Fund.

## What are the risks and what could I get in return?

The summary risk indicator of this product is **4 on a scale of 1 to 7**, which is a medium risk class. It rates the potential losses from future performance at a medium level. The recommended holding period is 5 years. If you cash in at an early stage, you may get back less than you invested.

Performance scenarios for an investment of 10,000 euros over 5 years:

| Scenario | After 1 year | After 5 years |
|---|---|---|
| Stress | 4,210 euros | 3,980 euros |
| Unfavourable | 8,350 euros | 8,920 euros |
| Moderate | 10,520 euros | 13,110 euros |
| Favourable | 13,940 euros | 17,560 euros |

## What are the costs?

- **Entry costs:** up to 3.00% of the amount invested. This is the maximum; your distributor may charge less.
- **Exit costs:** we do not charge an exit fee for this product.
- **Ongoing charges:** 1.65% of the value of your investment per year, covering management and administrative costs.
- **Transaction costs:** 0.18% per year, an estimate of the costs of buying and selling the underlying investments.
- **Performance fee:** 15% of the outperformance against the MSCI Europe Net Return index, with a high-water mark. No performance fee is due if the Fund underperforms its benchmark.

For an investment of 10,000 euros held for 5 years in the moderate scenario, total costs would amount to approximately 1,190 euros.

## How long should I hold it and can I take money out early?

Recommended holding period: 5 years. You can redeem your shares on any business day; see the dealing information for cut-off times.

## How can I complain?

Complaints can be sent to the compliance department of Northwind Asset Management by email at complaints@northwind-am.example.
