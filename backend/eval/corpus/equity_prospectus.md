# Aurora European Equity Fund — Prospectus (extract)

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform. Northwind Asset Management and the Aurora funds do not exist.*

## Management company

The Aurora European Equity Fund (the "Fund") is a sub-fund managed by Northwind Asset Management, a management company authorised in Luxembourg. The depositary bank is Harbour Trust Bank S.A. The Fund was launched on 14 March 2016 and its reference currency is the euro.

## Investment objective

The Fund aims to achieve long-term capital growth by investing in shares of European companies. It seeks to outperform its benchmark, the MSCI Europe Net Return index, over a rolling period of five years, after fees.

## Investment policy

The Fund invests at least 75% of its net assets in equities of companies headquartered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. Up to 10% of net assets may be invested in equities of companies from emerging markets. The Fund may hold up to 10% of its net assets in cash and money market instruments for liquidity purposes.

Exposure to a single issuer is limited to 8% of net assets. The portfolio usually holds between 50 and 70 companies. Stock selection relies on fundamental analysis of each company's balance sheet, cash flows and competitive position, combined with the ESG analysis described in the Fund's sustainability policy.

## Use of derivatives

Derivatives are used for hedging purposes and for efficient portfolio management only. The Fund does not use derivatives to create leverage: total market exposure may not exceed 100% of net assets. Currency risk on non-euro holdings may be partially hedged at the discretion of the portfolio manager.

## Securities lending

The Fund may lend up to 20% of its net assets to approved counterparties against collateral of at least 105% of the value of the securities lent. Revenues from securities lending, net of operational costs, are fully returned to the Fund.

## Main risks

- **Equity risk:** the value of shares can fall sharply, and investors may lose part of their capital.
- **Concentration risk:** investing in a limited number of companies increases the impact of any single company on performance.
- **Currency risk:** holdings in pounds sterling, Swiss francs or Nordic currencies can lose value against the euro.
- **Emerging market risk:** markets outside Europe may be less liquid and more volatile.
- **Sustainability risk:** an environmental, social or governance event could reduce the value of an investment.

The Fund is suitable for investors who can accept significant short-term fluctuations and who plan to stay invested for at least five years.
