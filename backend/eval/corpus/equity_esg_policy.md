# Aurora European Equity Fund — Sustainability Policy

*Fictional document written for the evaluation of the agent platform.*

## Classification

The Fund promotes environmental and social characteristics within the meaning of **Article 8 of the Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR)**. It does not have sustainable investment as its objective, but it commits to holding a minimum of 20% of sustainable investments.

## Exclusions

The Fund does not invest in companies that:

- derive more than 10% of their revenue from thermal coal mining or coal-fired power generation;
- are involved in controversial weapons, such as cluster munitions, anti-personnel mines, chemical or biological weapons;
- derive more than 5% of their revenue from tobacco production;
- are in severe breach of the principles of the United Nations Global Compact, without credible remediation.

The exclusion list is reviewed every quarter by the ESG committee of Northwind Asset Management.

## ESG integration

Each company is given an ESG score from 0 to 100 by the internal research team, combining data from two external providers with the analysts' own assessment. Companies scoring below 30 cannot be added to the portfolio. The weighted average ESG score of the portfolio must remain above that of the benchmark.

The carbon intensity of the portfolio, measured in tonnes of CO2 equivalent per million euros of revenue, must be at least 20% lower than that of the MSCI Europe Net Return index.

## Voting and engagement

Northwind Asset Management votes at the general meetings of all companies held in the portfolio. In 2025 the Fund voted at 64 general meetings and opposed at least one resolution at 41% of them, mostly on executive remuneration.

The management company runs an engagement programme with around 15 companies per year, focused on climate transition plans and board independence. If an engagement fails after two years, the company may be sold.
